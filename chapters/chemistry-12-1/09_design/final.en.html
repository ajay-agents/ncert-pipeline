Positive / negative deviation from Raoult's law and mixing enthalpy

What is meant by positive and negative deviations from Raoult's law and how is the sign of Δmix H related to positive and negative deviations from Raoult's law?

Given
By Raoult's law, each volatile component's partial vapour pressure in a solution is directly proportional to its mole fraction.
A solution that obeys Raoult's law at every concentration is called an ideal solution.
A solution that does not obey Raoult's law is non-ideal, and its vapour pressure is either higher or lower than Raoult's law predicts.
If the vapour pressure is higher, the solution shows positive deviation.
If it is lower, the solution shows negative deviation.
For an ideal solution, the enthalpy of mixing the pure components is zero.
Δsol H = 0
For a solution with positive deviation, heat is absorbed on mixing.
∴ Δsol H = Positive
For a solution with negative deviation, heat is released on mixing.
∴ Δsol H = Negative
